North Star

The corporate statesman

Chief Government Affairs Officer of a major global technology company — and then the encore: ambassador, boards, and the classroom.

The destination is chief of government affairs at a global technology company — most likely Cisco SVP Government Affairs — leading policy engagement across six continents on AI governance, cybersecurity, digital trade, and digital infrastructure. Hold it six to ten years, then exit corporate life in the mid-to-late 50s.

After that, three tracks run in sequence and then in parallel: a diplomatic appointment as ambassador, a portfolio of corporate and nonprofit boards, and teaching as a professor of practice. The influence of high office, without a ballot — built through a quarter century of substantive credibility rather than a campaign.

The archetype

Build durable credibility inside a global company, then convert it into diplomatic service, governance, and teaching. No ballot required.

Risk Management

Survive a two-year pause at any point

Career risks & hedges

  • Cisco institutional risk. The 20-year climb depends on Cisco's health — hedge through warm Zone B relationships at Microsoft, IBM, Apple, and Alphabet from the VP years.
  • Seat-timing risk. The Campbell seat may not open in window. Hedge: Zones B and G, plus the Zone C stint, which makes a mid-career external move stronger, not weaker.
  • Promotion velocity. Stalled more than two cycles at any tier — evaluate laterals seriously.
  • Appointment-cycle risk. Zones C, D, and F depend on who holds the White House. Mitigate with bipartisan credibility, relationships across both parties, Zone E/G alternatives, and patience.
  • Confirmation & vetting. Keep a clean quarter-century record: a clean digital footprint, CRS-style factual writing without partisan polemic, documented rationale for the Cisco positions you shape, and a clear personal position on legacy Cisco controversies (Doe v. Cisco, historical China allegations) developed early and held with integrity.

Personal risks

  • Aging parents & family emergencies. Build slack into every phase.
  • A partner's career. An overseas posting is a family decision years before it is a career decision.
  • Health events. The plan should survive a two-year pause at any point without collapsing — a design requirement, not a hope.

Life, Health & Identity

A marathon with no off-season
Physical

Training as infrastructure

Hybrid training — lifting plus conditioning — vegetarian nutrition planned around travel, 7–8 hours of sleep, disciplined caffeine and alcohol, and annual physical, dental, and mental-health check-ins. Non-negotiable.

Mental

Guarding against burnout

Executive and diplomatic careers carry high burnout and isolation rates. Standing therapy or coaching, a daily reflective practice, and a small circle of friends unconnected to ambition.

Identity

The foundation and the asset

Nigerian American identity is the personal foundation and, in the diplomatic lane, a professional asset that can't be manufactured later. Maintain Igbo fluency, invest in Nigeria family relationships, keep French sharp. Faith and values protected.

Relationships & family

Protected on their own terms

Regular calls, visits, and honored milestones. Partnership and family formation navigated on their own terms — joy and meaning, not a strategic variable. An overseas posting is a family decision years before it is a career decision.

Friendships

Unconnected to ambition

Three to five close friendships kept deliberately across decades — the people who have nothing to do with the climb.

Intellectual life

Forty books a year

Diplomatic memoirs and statecraft, AI-governance literature, African political economy, and negotiation — plus a daily private writing habit that becomes the public cadence and, eventually, the books.

Principles & Non-Negotiables

Four principles for the long arc

  1. Build the platform before deploying it. Everything before the encore is accumulation; deploy deliberately.
  2. Protect the T. AI governance and digital trade depth, on a federal and African base. Depth is what makes every zone want you.
  3. Every transition is a relationship event. From 2032 onward, the encore is decided by people who remember you from twenty years earlier.
  4. Stay rooted. Identity, family, faith, Igbo and French fluency, the hobbies. Quietly, these are also the qualifications.

Values

  • Substantive integrity. Take positions because you believe them — a diplomat's word is the entire asset.
  • Family and faith first. Three decades of climbing consume whatever is not actively protected.
  • Health as infrastructure. Training, nutrition, sleep, mental health. Non-negotiable.
  • Truthfulness as default. The leaders who hold up over decades default to the truth.
  • Community over self. The career is a platform; the service, boards, and classroom are what it's for.
